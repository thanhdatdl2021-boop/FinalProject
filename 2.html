Carbonade Flamanade - Belgium

Carbonade Flamanade - Belgium


Carbonade flamande ( Dutch : stoofvlees or stoverij , French : carbon(n)ade ) à la flamande is a stew made with beef (or pork ) and onions , popular in Belgium , the Netherlands and French Flanders , served with beer and mustard spread on bread, possibly spiced with bay leaves , thyme , cloves and juniper .
