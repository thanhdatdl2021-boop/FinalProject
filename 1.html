Pho - Vietnam

Pho is a traditional Vietnamese dish, considered one of the quintessential dishes of Vietnamese cuisine .


The main ingredients of pho are rice noodles and broth, along with thinly sliced ​​beef or chicken and onions. Beef pho broth is usually simmered from beef bones with spices such as cinnamon, star anise, and roasted cardamom. The best beef for pho is from local breeds (Vietnamese native cattle, yellow cattle). Other seasonings include soy sauce, pepper , lime , fish sauce , chili , etc. These are added according to personal taste. Pho is commonly eaten as a breakfast or late-night snack; however, in large cities, it can be enjoyed throughout the day. In the southern provinces of Vietnam and some other regions, pho is served with a plate of fresh herbs such as onions , bean sprouts , and cilantro , mint , and sawtooth coriander ; however, this plate of fresh herbs is usually absent in Hanoi . Pho is usually beef or chicken pho, but sometimes there are other variations, especially pho rolls, pho with wine sauce, dry pho, stir-fried pho, sour pho, duck pho in Cao Bang, and roasted pork pho in the northern mountainous provinces, and dry pho in Gia Lai.
